5th Wilmslow Scout Group

There's always something to get stuck into.

Scouting is about trying things, having adventures, making friends and learning skills that can help you throughout life.

Adventure for everyone

Do more. Try more. Discover your thing.

At 5th Wilmslow, our programme gives young people the chance to take part in fun indoor and outdoor activities, try new challenges and work together.

That could mean getting outdoors, learning practical skills, creating something, helping the community or simply having a great time with friends.

Scouts describes its programme through outcomes including adventure, citizenship, connectedness, leadership, wellbeing and Skills for Life. Activities can be adapted so young people of different ages and abilities can take part.

What we do

Adventure comes in all shapes and sizes.

There isn't one type of Scout activity. The national Scouts programme includes hundreds of activities covering outdoor skills, practical skills, physical activity, creativity, community engagement and more.

Camping & nights away

Put up tents, cook outdoors, spend time in nature and learn to become more independent. Young people can also help plan parts of a camp and take responsibility for different jobs.

Hiking & navigation

Get outside, explore new places and learn how to plan routes, navigate and look after yourself and your team along the way.

Water adventures

Depending on the programme and appropriate safety arrangements, Scouts can experience activities such as canoeing, kayaking and swimming.

Climbing & challenge

Take on physical challenges, build confidence and learn to keep going when something doesn't work first time.

Cooking & campfires

Learn practical skills such as preparing food, cooking outdoors and working safely around fires and camping equipment.

Creative activities

Scouting isn't all mud and tents. Young people can explore art, performance, photography, making and other creative challenges.

Team challenges

Work together, communicate, solve problems and take on challenges where everyone's contribution matters.

Community & helping others

Scouting encourages young people to care about their communities, take action and make a positive difference.

Skills for life

It's about what you learn along the way.

Scouts aims to help young people build skills they can use at school, at home, in future jobs and in everyday life.

Through activities and challenges, young people can practise being independent, communicating, problem solving, working in a team, taking responsibility and developing leadership skills.

Badges & progress

There are loads to discover.

Scouts has more than 200 badges covering a huge range of interests and achievements. They give young people another way to set goals, try something new and celebrate what they've learned.
