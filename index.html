Welcome

Welcome to 5th Wilmslow

We're a local Scout group based in Wilmslow, Cheshire, helping young people discover new experiences, develop skills and have fun along the way.

From camping and hiking to creative activities, water activities and team challenges, there's always something different to get involved with.
