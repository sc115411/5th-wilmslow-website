Frequently asked questions.

A few things parents often want to know before getting in touch.

How do I join?

Fill in the enquiry form below and we'll get back to you with information about the appropriate section and the next steps.

Do I need any previous experience?

No. Scouts is about trying new things, learning skills and having adventures. You don't need to have any previous experience.

What should I bring?

We'll let you know what you need for your first meeting. For most activities, comfortable clothing and suitable footwear are a good start.

Can parents help?

Absolutely. Volunteers are an important part of Scouts. If you'd like to help, mention it when you contact us.
