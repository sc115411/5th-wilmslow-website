Welcome to 5th Wilmslow

There is always something new to discover.

At 5th Wilmslow Scout Group, young people get the chance to try things they might never have tried before.

From getting outdoors and learning practical skills to taking on challenges with friends, Scouting is about having great experiences while building skills that last a lifetime.

New to Scouts?

Your first night is easy.

You do not need to know everything before you arrive. We will show you around, introduce you to everyone and help you get involved.

Step 01

Get in touch

Send us an enquiry and let us know a little about your child.

Step 02

Come along

We will arrange a time for you to come and see what we do.

Step 03

Give it a go

Join in with the activities and meet the rest of the group.

Step 04

Join the adventure

If you enjoy it, we can help you take the next step.

Adult volunteers

Could you help us?

Our volunteers make the adventures possible. You do not need to have been a Scout yourself, and you do not need to be an expert in outdoor adventure.

There are lots of different ways to get involved, and we are always happy to hear from people who would like to help young people enjoy great experiences.
